Practice · UPDATE & DELETE

Change and remove records safely

UPDATE changes values in existing records; DELETE removes records. Both are irreversible, and both live or die by the WHERE clause. In this practice you fix names and prices and clear out rows — exactly the class-practice edits — without ever touching a row you did not mean to.
