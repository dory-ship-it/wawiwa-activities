Practice 3 · Correlated subquery & EXISTS

What does this query return?

SELECT c.Name FROM Customers c WHERE EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM Orders o WHERE o.CustID = c.ID)

EXISTS is true when the inner query finds at least one matching row. The inner query uses c.ID from the outer — a correlated subquery.
