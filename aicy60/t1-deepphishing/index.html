Assignments Description

This Assignment should be done in small teams of 2-4.

You’ve been hired to perform a red-team simulation on a local pizzeria. Research the business, relevant health regulations, and craft a convincing inspection request that could persuade staff to download a file in a controlled test environment.

Good luck!

Requirements and Limitations. This is an individual assignment. If you require assistance, consult any reference materials specified in this document (above) or address the instructor. Please avoid “sharing” solutions (or “hints”) between your peers. Time limits or additional requirements / limitations will be provided by the instructor should it be necessary. Everything here is a controlled, authorised training simulation — never target a real business or person.
Before you write. Do not enter real names, ID numbers, phone numbers, emails, passwords, or other sensitive data in your answers — generalise instead. When you ask the coach for feedback, your text is anonymised before it is sent, and nothing is stored.
