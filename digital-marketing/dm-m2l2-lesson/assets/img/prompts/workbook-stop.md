# Image slot: workbook-stop

Status: PLACEHOLDER in Phase 1 (assets/img/temp/workbook-stop.jpg is a plain gradient, temp: true). Noy chooses the final direction from Hugging Face options; the real image then replaces the file and temp becomes false.

## Where it is used
Every workbook stop (WB0, WB1, WB2, WB3 and later WB4): the picture inside the panel, and the blurred ground behind it. One image for all stops, so the learner recognises the stop at once.

## Subject
A laptop open on a marketer's desk; on its screen a workbook page: a clean form with a few stacked input blocks and one filled bar (abstract, nothing readable). A notebook and a pen beside it, a coffee cup out of focus.

## People
Optional: one hand resting near the trackpad, no face.

## Placement
The laptop centred, seen from slightly above; the crop inside the panel is 4:3 from the centre.

## Shared photographic language (the same for every image of this course)
Realistic editorial photography of digital marketing work, as in a business magazine feature.
A marketer's real desk and tools: a laptop or monitor with a dashboard, search results, a phone with a social feed, printed charts, a notebook.
Natural window light, calm and bright. Palette: muted teal, deep navy, warm stone and off-white; one small warm accent at most.
Shallow depth of field, 35 to 50 mm look, slightly from above or at desk level. Tidy, uncluttered, room to breathe.
People only where this slot says so; then hands or a side view, natural, no posed stock smile, no eye contact with the camera.
Rules: no text in the image (no words, letters, digits, captions or UI labels: screens show abstract charts, bars and blocks only), no real brands, no logos, no watermarks, no illustration, no 3D render, no hologram or glowing network lines.
Format: 16:9, 1920 x 1080, JPG. The subject sits where the slot says, so the lesson's panel never covers it.
