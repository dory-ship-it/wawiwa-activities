## Where it is used
Every workbook stop (WB0 to WB4) and the home practice (S36): the picture inside the panel and the blurred ground behind it. One image for all stops, so the learner recognises the stop at once.

## Subject
The same laptop-and-notebook frame every time: the laptop on the packing table showing a form with stacked input blocks and one filled bar (abstract), an open notebook and a pen beside it, two plain bottles out of focus behind.

## People
Optional: one hand near the trackpad, no face.

## Placement
The laptop centred, seen from slightly above; the crop inside the panel is 4:3 from the centre.

## Shared setting (the same for every image of this lesson, and for its films V1 and V2)
One place: the bright studio of a small direct-to-consumer skincare brand. A packing table, plain unlabelled bottles and jars, kraft parcels, a laptop whose online store is blurred, a phone on a small tripod.
Hands at work. No faces, no names, no logos, no text in the image (no words, letters, digits or UI labels: screens show blurred blocks and abstract charts only).
Realistic editorial photography, natural window light, calm and bright. Palette: muted teal, deep navy, warm stone, kraft brown and off-white; one small warm accent at most.
Shallow depth of field, 35 to 50 mm look, slightly from above or at table level. Tidy, uncluttered, room to breathe.
Rules: near-sharp (the lesson adds its own light blur where needed), no real brands, no watermarks, no illustration, no 3D render, no glowing network lines.
Format: 16:9, 1920 x 1080, JPG. The subject sits where the slot says, so the lesson's panel never covers it.
