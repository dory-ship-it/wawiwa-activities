## Shared photographic language (the same for every image of this course)

A marketer's real desk and tools: a laptop or monitor with a dashboard, search results, a phone with a social feed, printed charts, a notebook.
Natural window light, calm and bright. Palette: muted teal, deep navy, warm stone and off-white; one small warm accent at most.
Shallow depth of field, 35 to 50 mm look, slightly from above or at desk level. Tidy, uncluttered, room to breathe.

Rules: no text in the image (no words, letters, digits, captions or UI labels: screens show abstract charts, bars and blocks only), no real brands, no logos, no watermarks, no illustration, no 3D render, no hologram or glowing network lines.
