## 5. Content and clarity issues per chapter

These are my own observations, so the quotes are the lesson's words, not a human tester's.

**Chapter 1 Intro**
- Decide (Dana): the right-answer reveal says requests "arrive by WhatsApp, email and in person, with no organized system", but the question only said "about 90 requests a month, most of them repeat questions". The learner is judged on facts not yet shown. Picking "Survey employees" gets no explanation of why it is wrong.
- "Show me how" sits right under Dana's quote, so it reads like "show me how to put a chatbot". It only replays the hint animation.
- Write: there are two save buttons ("Save" in the card and "Save to continue" at the bottom right). The problem box allows 250 characters, which is tight for "Today X happens, and it hurts because Y".

**Chapter 2 Why map**
- Warm-up: the answer was already given by the start screen's tagline ("Don't accelerate a broken process"), so the warm-up is too easy.
- Decide: "No success metric" is the correct second cause, but the feedback says "both were judged by numbers". If both had numbers, the missing metric was not the cause. The car-company story never shows a missing metric. Logic issue.
- Two slogans for one idea: the video says "Don't Automate, Obliterate" and the key-idea screen says "Don't accelerate. Deconstruct."
- Key idea: "With mapping: Fewer steps first" clashes with the later rule "map AS-IS, don't fix while mapping". Mapping reveals steps; redesign removes them.
- Small items: "fintech, 2024" (screen) vs "2024 to 2025" (video); "Five times fewer" reads awkwardly.

**Chapter 3 Toolkit (9 steps, the longest chapter)**
- Order of teaching: the Decide screen asks about the "Field template: eight fields" before the eight fields are introduced (Explore comes next), and there is no screen that teaches when to use the template and when to use BPMN, only a hint line.
- Spot tests the three AS-IS rules ("Don't fix", "Don't add AI", "Don't map the official process"), but they are only taught in the Chapter 4 video ("No fixing, no AI", 0:26).
- Explore: the cards are an accordion, and after the 8th card opens the screen turns straight into a quiz. The field definitions can no longer be reread. The cards have no numbers, yet later feedback refers to "Fields 4 and 7".
- Too easy: Sort repeats the Learn screen just shown, Label shows exactly where each label goes on the same diagram as the previous screen, and the Explore question is answered by the one card that is open.
- BPMN diagram: "Escalate" sits in the HR manager lane, but HR staff escalate. The manager's task would be "answer the escalated question".
- Warm-ups end with "Hold that thought" and never say what the right answer was.

**Chapter 4 HR demo**
- Content error: the completed HR map, Field 3, says "Receiving the request (employee)", and the video's lane diagram puts "Receive" in the Employee lane. The employee sends the request; HR staff receive it. Chapter 5 itself says "HR staff open the inbox".
- Stop 1 in the video (Field 2) is the same question as Chapter 3 Spot round 2.
- Measure: the answer (35% now, 20% in 3 months) is given word for word in the video 30 seconds earlier. A new idea, "Guardrail: answer accuracy stays at or above 95% in a QA sample", appears with no explanation. It also clashes with Fields 6 and 7 (no records and no control today, so nobody can sample accuracy).
- Field 5 Sort: the correct-answer feedback is generic. It does not say why "No formal control" is Field 6.

**Chapter 5 Critical eye** (visuals not checked)
- The Learn screen "Three good fits" teaches where AI fits. That overlaps Lesson 5's stated job ("next lesson marks on your map where AI enters"). **To confirm** with the Lesson 5 owner.
- Sort: "Tag free-text questions by topic" (unstructured to structured) and "Label requests urgent or routine" (low-risk decision) are both classification tasks, so the line between them is thin. "Summarize before escalating" also fits "unstructured to structured". A learner who swaps them is marked wrong.

- Good: the Write screen carries my Chapter 3 steps forward, and "This note stays off your AS-IS map" handles the don't-add-AI tension well.

**Chapter 6 Build yours** (visuals not checked)
- Match: the cards A to D are in the same order as the fields top to bottom, so the answer is A, B, C, D.
- The workbook does not match the lesson:
  - Field 5 sensitivity tags in the workbook are "public, internal, sensitive, personal, customer, employee, financial". The lesson taught four levels: PUBLIC, INTERNAL, CONFIDENTIAL, RESTRICTED. The coach then advised me to add "sensitive".

  - The coach says "Strong work..." and then labels the field "Readiness: Light", which is confusing.
- Next on the workbook step is not locked, so a learner can skip the workbook and the PDF and still finish.
- Good: the copy panel with all my earlier notes, the coach's specific questions, and the 3-question self-check where "Not yet" gives one fix to make.

- The in-video stops are well timed ("Before you see what they found, make the first call yourself"), and the transcript and CC are available.
- The keyboard path for every drag task works well.
- Feedback in Spot, Record, Flag and the Knowledge check is specific and explains the consequence ("Lesson 5 would rely on it").
- Two real cases (car company, fintech) with numbers, and the honest note "for learning, not as proof that people should be replaced".
- The workbook coach gives specific, useful questions.
- The self-check ("Not yet gives you one fix") and the "Welcome back / Resume" dialog.
- Privacy reminders are consistent on every input screen.

## 8. Top 5 changes (by impact on the learner)

1. **Teach before you test.** Move the AS-IS rules before Chapter 3 Spot, introduce the eight fields before the template-vs-BPMN question, and add a short Learn screen for the five mistakes before Chapter 5 Spot.
2. **Align the workbook with the lesson.** Use the same four sensitivity levels, add the date to Field 8, remove "in class", make the coach and the rubric agree, and make the unlock behaviour visible (Bug 2).
3. **Fix the Chapter 3 Label drop target** (Bug 1). The "Drop here" tag must accept the drop.
4. **Fix the content errors:** Field 3 "Receiving the request (employee)", the Chapter 2 "No success metric" logic, and the Chapter 1 Dana reveal that uses facts the learner never saw.
5. **Raise the challenge.** Many items are answered by the hint, the card order or keyword matching (Ch3 Sort, Label and Explore; Ch4 Measure; Ch5 Spot; Ch6 Match). Replace some of them with transfer questions on a new case or on the learner's own case.

**7/10.** The flow is solid and carrying my own case into the workbook works very well, but several screens test things before they are taught, and the workbook uses slightly different terms than the lesson.
