Class practice: the product polish sprint

Module 4 · Vibe Coding  ·  Lesson 4  ·  Stage A 20 min, Stage B 20 min

In pairs, on a tool one of you built in Lesson 2 or Lesson 3. 45 minutes. The last five are for the report-back, not for building. Dummy data only, and nothing confidential in the screenshots.
Stage A — audit, 20 minutes
01

Before, and the one flow

Capture a before screenshot of the main screen. Then name the single flow the tool exists to support.

A tool has one job. Naming it is what stops the sprint turning into redecorating.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
02

The audit prompt, run unchanged

Run the UX audit prompt exactly as written and read what comes back. Do not fix anything yet.

Unchanged matters: the prompt is written to analyse and report without touching code, and that is what makes the report trustworthy.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
03

The three findings worth fixing

Add anything your own practical-stop findings caught that the audit missed. Then agree the three worth fixing, and why those three.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
Stage B — fix and verify, 20 minutes
04

Fix, one described behaviour at a time

Ask for one behaviour at a time and test after each. Then check the result on a laptop, at phone width, and in your language.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
05

The four sentences, and what you did not fix

Prepare the report-back on one fix: the fault, who it hurt, what you changed, why it is better. Then keep every finding you did not fix.

The list you chose not to fix is the handover note. It is the half of this practice people throw away.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
