Class practice: the same task in two tools

Module 4 · Vibe Coding  ·  Lesson 1  ·  Stage A 20 min, Stage B 25 min

Dummy data only. No personal details and no confidential material, in either tool. You need a device and two AI chat assistants you can sign into.
Stage A — individual work, 20 minutes
01

The task, the deliverable, the audience

Choose one work task you would really hand to a language model. Name what comes out of it and who reads it.

A deliverable, not a topic. “A one-page summary for my team lead” is a deliverable; “something about onboarding” is not.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
02

One prompt, and it is the same string in both tools

Write the prompt you will run. Do not change a word between the two tools — that is the whole point of the comparison.

Four things shape the result: what you ask for, what the model needs to know, how the answer should look, and how you will improve it.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
03

What came back, unedited

Run it in both tools and keep both answers as they came out. Here, write what actually differed between them.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
Stage B — pairs, 25 minutes
04

One improvement round, in both tools

Ask each tool to critique its own answer. Then improve once, and once only, in both. Name the defect you were fixing.

“Make it better” is not an improvement round. One change, stated, so you can tell whether it worked.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
05

The comparison, and the verdict

Score both tools on the three criteria, then write the verdict in two sentences. It is a winner for this task only.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
