Class practice: specify first, then build the core

Module 4 · Vibe Coding  ·  Lesson 3  ·  Stage A 20 min, Stage B 25 min

Work on one idea only. Bring the business process you mapped in Module 1 or Module 2 if you have one, and use dummy data throughout.
Stage A — individual work, 20 minutes
01

The eight questions, on your idea

Answer all eight on one idea only. Short answers are fine; missing answers are not.

Question 1 is the one that decides the rest: if you cannot name one central person, you are not ready to name a problem.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
02

Core, Supporting, Future

Sort the capabilities into the three tiers. Today you build the Core only.

If everything is Core, nothing is. The tiers exist so the first build can finish.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
03

The data model

One entity, then every field on it: the name, its type, whether it is required, and its default.

Entity is the thing you track. Fields are what you record about it. Type is what kind of value each field holds.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
Stage B — pairs, 25 minutes
04

Acceptance criteria, as yes-or-no checks

Write the checks that decide whether the build worked. Each one has to be answerable yes or no by looking at the screen.

“It works well” cannot be checked. “It cannot be saved without a name” can.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
05

The first prompt, and what the platform decided

Translate the specification into a first prompt and build the Core only, one step at a time. Then run your partner through your criteria.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
