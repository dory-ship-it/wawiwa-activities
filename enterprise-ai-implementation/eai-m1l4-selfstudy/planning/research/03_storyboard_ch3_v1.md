Facts used: only the 01_analyst fact sheet and HR CASE CANON. Flags cited by their 01_analyst IDs (FL#).

**Chapter 3 sequence:** V2 "Mapping the HR case" (video scriptwriter) → S09 Sort → S10 Decision → S11 Decision → S12 Explorer.

**Division of labour with V2:** V2 builds fields 1 to 8 live and explains what each field means. S09 to S12 do not re-explain fields. They put the learner in the mapper's seat: place, choose, check. Word counts include the instruction line and every word on the stage card in its default state. Numerals and symbols (~, %, ≥) count as one word each when they stand alone. Feedback is counted separately (max 25).

1. **S09 reshaped to "Sort Field 5 parts: source / owner / sensitivity / not Field 5".** No source assigns sensitivity tags to sample items, so a tag-sort key would be invented (FL6).

3. **S12 gets a closing check question (tiles as answers).** Keeps the worked example from being a read-only screen (rule 8) and previews S14 handoffs.
4. **V2 stops should test Fields 2 to 4 (trigger, steps, handoffs), not Fields 5 to 8.** S09 to S11 own those decisions; repeating them would duplicate practice.
5. **SME: define or drop "QA sample".** It appears on s20 with no definition, so no tooltip can be sourced.
6. **SME: confirm the Field 8 target reading (FL8) and the tag vocabulary (FL6)** before S11 feedback and S09 card 4 are locked.
