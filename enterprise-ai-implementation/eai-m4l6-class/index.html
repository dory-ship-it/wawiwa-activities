Class practice: connect your own project to the world

Module 4 · Vibe Coding  ·  Lesson 6  ·  Stage A 10 min, Stage B 35 min

In your project pairs. 45 minutes. Dummy data only, and no real key of any kind, in any project. The target is one thing that genuinely works by the end.
Stage A — decide, 10 minutes
01

What your project should reach for

What outside information or ability would actually improve your project, and which service would it come from?

Start from the need, not from the service. “It would be useful to connect something” is not a need.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
02

Ready-made, or direct?

Check whether your platform already has a ready-made connection for it. That check comes before anything else.

A pair that skips this check spends half the practice building something that already existed.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
03

What should happen on its own, and does it need a key?

Name the trigger and the action you want. Then answer the key question: does this service need to know who is asking?

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
Stage B — build, 35 minutes
04

Build it, and watch it work

One integration or one automation, working in your own project. Then test it. A connection you have not watched work is not done.

Describe the behaviour you want, one change at a time, exactly as in Lesson 2.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
05

What the platform decided, and what failed

One thing the platform decided that you had not asked for. And one thing that failed, with what the message actually said.

A failure with a message you can quote is better evidence of understanding than a first-time success.

Keep it about the work. No names, email addresses, phone numbers or anything else personal, yours or anyone else’s.
