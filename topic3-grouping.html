Practice 2 · COUNT(*) vs COUNT(column)

South has two orders, one with a NULL amount. What do the counts return?

SELECT COUNT(*), COUNT(Amount) FROM Orders WHERE Region = 'South'

South has 2 rows; one has an Amount, the other is NULL.
