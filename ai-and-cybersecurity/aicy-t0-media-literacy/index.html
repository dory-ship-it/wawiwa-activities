Media Literacy

In the assignment, the student will evaluate their ability to detect AI-generated media, including deepfakes, realistic faces, videos, artwork, and chat interactions, by testing their skills across various online identification tools.

  1. Go to detectfakes.kellogg.northwestern.edu And try the deep fake detection test, see how many you can detect!

  2. Try do detect real faces on https://whichfaceisreal.com/

  3. Try to detect AI videos on https://sightengine.com/ai-or-not-video

  4. Try to identify AI art on https://quillbot.com/blog/human-or-ai-game/

  5. See if you can detect if youre talking to humans or AI https://humanornot.so/
